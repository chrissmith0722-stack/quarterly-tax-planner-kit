# CPA / EA Year-End Handoff Packet

Give your tax pro a clean packet — not a Slack dump.

## Export checklist
- [ ] Annual income-by-source CSV (all quarters)
- [ ] Schedule C–style expense rollup CSV
- [ ] Mileage log CSV + the mileage rate you used
- [ ] Quarterly payments log (federal + state) with confirmation IDs
- [ ] Platform 1099-K / 1099-NEC when they arrive
- [ ] Prior-year return PDF (if they didn't prepare it)
- [ ] Notes: home-office method choice, health insurance, retirement contributions, estimated payments already made

## One-paragraph brief (fill in)
> I'm a solo freelancer / digital seller. Income is mostly [clients / Gumroad / Stripe]. I tracked monthly cash in [Cashflow Kit / other] and quarterly estimates with this planner. Please review SE tax, quarterly safe harbor, and state obligations for [TAX_YEAR]. Open questions: [LIST].

## What this kit is not
- Not a filed Schedule C
- Not audited books
- Not advice — organization for professionals to review
